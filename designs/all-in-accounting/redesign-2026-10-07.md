## Contract and interpretation

- Currency, item, service, measure unit and operating unit are typed descriptors.
  Organization, treasury, direct tax, indirect tax, stock, miscellaneous stock
  and miscellaneous currency records are actual accounts, each with a required
  currency and independent real/receivable/payable history. Monetary and resource
  units are never summed together. Remove the entity/claim account wrappers.
- Account records explicitly join descriptor indexes. Stock uniqueness is
  `(currency, resource, operating_unit)` across both stock table families and
  excludes effective time. Chronological indexes are separate from uniqueness.
  These memberships index accounts; they do not roll balances up a second time.
- Invoices are validators, with required organization and `invoice_date`.
  Empty invoices are valid. Sales/purchase issues require their matching invoice.
  Each fact explicitly participates in every relevant account and validator.
- Original issues and taxes occur on/before `invoice_date`. Advance cash can
  precede the issue's effective time, but the complete invoice-date balance must
  cover it. Every subsequent complete timestamp must also be solvent. A later
  source mutation cannot rely on a later statement to repair its invalid closure.
- Refunds/returns occur on/before the invoice date; adjustment records occur
  after it. A negative payment adjustment is the cash reversal after that date.
  This resolves the request's conflicting use of the word refund without losing
  the operation. The user confirmed this date-based split on October 7.
- An explicit positive exchange rate converts source currency to destination
  currency; equal currencies require exactly one. Derived mirrors of a selected
  source rate are not a second independent quote. Native decimal arithmetic and
  currency rounding remain mandatory.
- A base tax consumes the issue's dated base. A net tax consumes the issue's
  strictly earlier net aggregate and has only one issue parent. Equal-time net
  taxes are parallel; use distinct effective times for compounding. Neither reads
  a final aggregate containing itself. Tax rules are bounded percentage slopes.
- Quantity, price and exchange-rate corrections are explicit signed marginal
  changes to the original issue, with one dimension per correction record.
  Source quantity/value capacity guards prevent excessive reversals. Positive
  deltas represent debit corrections. Physical quantity corrections reverse stock
  as well as the monetary claim. Price/rate corrections change only value.
- Adjustment-tax rows require both their correction and the original tax. They
  follow the original rule reactively, and can consume earlier adjustment taxes
  for a net-tax reversal. Their effective order must avoid taxing a correction
  twice; no matching child rows are automatically created.
- Production consists of an ordinary parent plus independently authored input,
  output, cash-cost and capacity rows. Input reservation and later consumption,
  anticipated output and later receipt, and interval capacity use multiple slots
  on the same source record. Gross start-time demand must be available strictly
  before production starts; same-time outputs cannot manufacture their own input.
- Tenant authorization, reader sets, the synchronous refresh coordinator and
  native lookup indexes retain their established contracts. New analytics trees
  are explicit schema choices. No Rust runtime, formula DSL or recipe generator
  is needed for this request.
