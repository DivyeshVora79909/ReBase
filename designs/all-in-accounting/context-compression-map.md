## Contradiction and preservation notes

- Older core documents deferred domain expansion; later user direction reopened
  accounting, logistics, exchange, and basic assembly. Current authority is
  explained in the context map and blueprint.
- Older organization/book wording is superseded by the direct user/organization
  identity model. Authorization ownership remains separate; H1 evidence is
  bounded and does not establish populated migration.
- Passing fixtures remain bounded evidence. They do not establish production
  migration, live providers, statutory tax interpretation, physical cost, or
  representative load.
- Core implementation/restart details (shared scheduling horizon and cadence,
  atomic Redis admission boundary, age-only dead-letter retention, credential
  ownership/use boundary, final integration checks, dirty-tree preservation,
  and focused-check discipline) remain in `core-handoff.md`.
- The accounting handoff, blueprints, plans, core audit, verification, evidence,
  source code, and research were not modified by this slice.
