The useful primitives in the user's examples are:

- A record supplies typed identity and scalar facts. A sparse account adds only
  the dimensions actually used, rather than materializing their Cartesian product.
- A root collects an ordered vector of contributions. A source can participate
  in several roots; being a parent does not automatically enroll it in a tree.
- A root can validate, publish aggregates, or also participate elsewhere. These
  are independent roles. An invoice groups and validates its participants; it
  does not repeat their postings to the organization or subsidiary.
- The meaning and unit of each component are explicit. Some roots use only real
  movements, while others also use receivable/payable components.
- A linear constraint is maintained as its own per-contribution linear measure.
  Its complete-time minimum/maximum validates every boundary. Separate minima
  cannot be subtracted to validate a relationship between two time series.
- A calculation may read a direct field, an ordered prefix, a predecessor value,
  or a complete aggregate. Reading through a function does not itself explain
  which records to refresh when the function's source changes.

The latest request supersedes the old single-currency organization identity,

Existing explicit fees, taxes, transfers, independent claims, manufacturing and

## Model decisions to prove

1. `subsidiary` is a business boundary within the existing authorization tenant,
   not a new authentication root. Organizations and owned/tax accounts belong to
   one subsidiary. Cross-subsidiary movements require explicit paired legs.
2. An organization has its reporting currency and sparse native currency/resource
   positions. Owned stock is identified by subsidiary, resource and operating
   unit, independently of pricing currency. Several treasuries can hold the same
   currency; their record identities distinguish actual cash locations.
3. Native histories preserve currency amounts or resource quantities. Reporting
   trees convert contributions into their root's currency. They never sum raw
   quantities of unrelated resources or unconverted currencies.
4. Counterparty positions use the counterparty's perspective; subsidiary claims
   use the mirrored perspective. Owned real, counterparty real and tax movements
   remain distinct reporting components so consolidation does not count both
   sides as owned assets.
5. Receivable/payable contributions are nonnegative directional accumulators.
   A correction or settlement adds to the opposing side. Their difference is
   the outstanding position, not two independently decreasing balances. Signed
   real movements and signed auxiliary capacity measures remain ordinary algebra.
6. Invoice capacity is a linear measure of obligation minus settlement. Covered
   advances retain the previously requested invoice-date boundary; afterwards
   every complete timestamp must be valid. Empty documents are valid. A unified
   adjustment follows its source and is not split into return/refund tables by
   the invoice date. Notes enforce consistent dimensions and direction without
   a draft/finalization workflow.
7. An issue adjustment changes the dated quantity/price state. Its monetary delta
   is the difference between the resulting products, with native decimal rounding.
   It is not an accidental sum of independent marginal changes when both inputs
   change. Original and cumulative reversal capacity must both remain valid.
8. Base tax, net tax and the distinct adjustment calculations use strict tables
   and explicit references. Original rules propagate directly; dated bases use
   the temporal protocol. Explicit adjustment children must not duplicate a change
   already included in an original tax's dated basis. Capped rules use marginal
   differences, not simply the rule applied to a negative adjustment amount.
9. A directed exchange pair is shared across subsidiaries within the tenant. Its
   initial rate and uniquely dated rate records define a piecewise constant
   function. Each calculation selects the last rate at or before its own date.
   Observed trade prices are separate from authoritative rates, avoiding feedback.
10. Manufacturing retains explicit start/end positions and capacity reservations.
    Owned treasury/stock R/P remain unused; reservations and expected output use
    separately named measures. Future receipts cannot fund earlier consumption.
